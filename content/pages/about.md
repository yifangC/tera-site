# **Yi-Fang** Cheng

Hi there! Welcome to my personal webpage.

I'm a fifth-year Ph.D. student with a graduate certificate in Applied Statistics at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu)'s Lingusitics Department, where I also earned my MA in Linguistics. I work with Prof. [Daniel J. Olson](https://danieljolson.weebly.com) in [Purdue Bilingualism Lab (PBL)](https://purduebilingualismlab.weebly.com). I'm interested in the intersection of computational linguistics and phonetics, particularly in bilingualism.

My dissertation, supported by the Taiwanese Overseas Pioneers Grants from the [National Science and Technology Council](https://www.nstc.gov.tw/?l=en), focuses on the temporal dynamics of cross-linguistic phonetic interference. As bilinguals manage multiple languages within a single cognitive system, these are some of the questions I’m trying to answer using both lab-based experimental designs and computational approaches on neural network models:

- How do bilingual speakers' languages interact with each other?
- How do bilingual speakers’ acoustic patterns shift as they switch between languages (code-switch)?
- How can these moment-to-moment acoustic changes be measured and modeled to better understand the cognitive mechanisms underlying bilingual speech production?

I also work with Prof. [Elsayed Issa](https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~esissa/) in [Computational Linguistic @ Purdue (CLAP)](https://www.cla.purdue.edu/research/clai/index.html) on Human–AI interaction. Our work on AI in language learning is supported by the [NSF National I-Corps program](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps).

I received my Bachelor's degrees in both Music and English from [National Taiwan Normal University](https://en.ntnu.edu.tw). I worked previously as a pianist before joining Purdue.
